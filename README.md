# The Hunger Games

## Overview
**The Hunger Games** is a dystopian action-adventure film series set in the nation of *Panem*, where the Capitol forces districts to send tributes into an annual televised survival arena.

> "May the odds be ever in your favor."

---

### Main Film Sequence (Ordered List)
1. The Hunger Games
2. The Hunger Games: Catching Fire
3. The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1
4. The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2

### Why It Stands Out (Unordered List)
- **Katniss Everdeen** is a compelling protagonist whose choices drive the rebellion.
- *Survival tension* pairs smoothly with themes of media manipulation and power.
- District `12` sets the grounded starting point before the story scales up across Panem.
